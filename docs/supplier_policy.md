# Supplier Policy (synthetic example)

## Payment terms
Standard payment terms with all suppliers are net 30 days from the invoice date. Alpha Traders offers a 2% discount for payment within 10 days.

## Lead times
Quoted lead times are 3 days for Alpha Traders, 5 days for Crescent Supply, 7 days for Beta Wholesale and 10 days for Delta Imports. Delta Imports shipments may be delayed by customs clearance.

## Supplier performance reviews
Suppliers are reviewed every quarter on on-time delivery and defect rate. A supplier with more than 10% late deliveries in a quarter is placed on a watch list.
