# Reorder Rules (synthetic example)

## Reorder quantity
Reorder quantity is the expected demand over the supplier lead time plus a safety stock of 7 days of average demand. Round up to the supplier's minimum order size.

## Reorder trigger
A product is reordered when stock falls below its reorder level, or when the demand forecast shows a stockout within the supplier lead time.

## Emergency purchase orders
Emergency purchase orders must be approved by the inventory manager. Orders above 100,000 PKR also require finance approval.

## Stock counts
A full physical stock count is done every quarter. Cycle counts of fast-moving items are done weekly.
