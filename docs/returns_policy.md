# Returns Policy (synthetic example)

## Damaged goods
Customers may return damaged goods within 14 days of delivery for a full refund. A photo of the damage is required.

## Change of mind
Unopened items can be returned within 7 days of purchase for store credit. Grocery items cannot be returned once delivered.

## Supplier returns
Defective stock is returned to the supplier within 5 working days of receipt. Credit notes are issued by the supplier within 15 days.
